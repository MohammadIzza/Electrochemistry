Established in 2025, the Research Cluster for Electrochemistry (RCEC) serves as a collaborative platform for advancing innovative research in electrochemistry. Since its establishment, RCEC has focused on strengthening interdisciplinary connections, developing new research directions, and expanding the contribution of electrochemical science to fundamental understanding and practical applications.

Widely recognized as one of the defining figures of modern electrochemistry, Allen J. Bard devoted more than five decades of his academic career to The University of Texas at Austin. Through groundbreaking research, technological innovation, and sustained mentorship, he helped establish UT Austin as a major center for electrochemical science and trained generations of researchers who later became leaders in the field.
