Bioelectrochemistry focuses on the integration of biological systems with electrochemical technologies to develop innovative solutions in biosensing, bioenergy, biotechnology, and healthcare. Research encompasses the interaction between biomolecules, microorganisms, enzymes, and electrode surfaces, including biological electron transfer and bioelectrochemical interfaces. The cluster aims to translate fundamental bioelectrochemical understanding into practical technologies for energy, health, environmental, and biotechnological applications.

## Key Research Areas

- Microbial and enzymatic electrochemistry
- Biosensors and bioelectronic interfaces
- Microbial fuel cells and bioelectrochemical systems
- Biological electron transfer and extracellular electron transfer
- Enzyme-based electrochemical devices
- Electrochemical technologies for healthcare and biotechnology
