Photoelectrochemistry focuses on the integration of light-responsive materials and electrochemical systems for sustainable energy conversion and storage. Research in this cluster explores semiconductor materials, photoelectrodes, charge-transfer processes, and light-driven electrochemical reactions. The research aims to develop efficient technologies for solar energy conversion, hydrogen production, carbon conversion, and other clean-energy applications.

## Key Research Areas

- Semiconductor and photoelectrode materials
- Light-driven electrochemical reactions
- Photoelectrochemical water splitting
- Solar-to-chemical energy conversion
- Hydrogen production and renewable fuels
- Charge separation and interfacial charge transfer
