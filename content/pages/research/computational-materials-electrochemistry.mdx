<Text>

Computational Electrochemistry develops computational and data-driven approaches to understand, predict, and optimize electrochemical systems. Research combines molecular simulation, density functional theory (DFT), multiphysics modeling, machine learning, and artificial intelligence to investigate reaction mechanisms, material properties, transport phenomena, and electrochemical interfaces. The cluster supports the rational design of advanced electrochemical materials and systems while reducing the reliance on trial-and-error experimentation.

## Key Research Areas

- Density functional theory and molecular simulations
- Electrochemical reaction mechanism modeling
- Multiphysics and transport modeling
- Machine learning and artificial intelligence for electrochemistry
- Computational materials design
- Data-driven prediction and optimization of electrochemical system
