Electrocatalysis & Electroanalysis focuses on the development of electrochemical catalysts, electrode materials, and advanced analytical techniques. Research in this cluster covers electrocatalytic reaction mechanisms, interfacial processes, electrode–electrolyte interactions, and electrochemical reaction kinetics. The cluster also develops electrochemical methods and devices for chemical and environmental analysis, with applications in energy, sensing, and sustainable technologies.

## Key Research Areas

- Electrocatalytic materials and reaction mechanisms
- Electrode and electrode–electrolyte interfaces
- Electrochemical kinetics and reaction pathways
- Advanced electrochemical characterization
- Electrochemical sensors and analytical devices
- Environmental and energy-related electroanalysis
