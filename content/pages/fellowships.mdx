<Text>

Each semester, Bard CEC awards 2-3 Graduate Student Fellowships to support and promote innovative and collaborative electrochemical research within the Bard CEC Affiliate community. These fellowships aim to advance impactful projects, foster collaboration, and produce research outcomes that highlight the productivity of Bard CEC's activities.

We gratefully acknowledge the sponsors who make our **Graduate Student Fellowship** series possible: the <strong>Welch Foundation (Grant No. H-F-0037)</strong>, the **CH Instruments–Bard CEC Research Fellowship**, and the **Bard Family CEC Fellowship**.

</Text>
