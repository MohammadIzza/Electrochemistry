## Founding Mission

The Research Cluster for Electrochemistry (RCEC) was established in 2025 to build upon the growing expertise and research strengths in electrochemistry at Universitas Diponegoro, particularly within the Faculty of Science and Mathematics.

Our mission is to advance fundamental and applied research involving the transfer of electrons and ions across interfaces and to translate this knowledge into solutions for scientific and technological challenges. By closely integrating electrochemistry with materials science and related disciplines, RCEC provides a multidisciplinary environment for research with applications ranging from energy conversion and storage to healthcare, sensing, environmental technologies, and emerging functional materials.

RCEC brings together faculty members, research staff, and students with expertise spanning chemistry, physics, biology and biotechnology, materials science, mathematics, statistics, informatics, and other areas relevant to electrochemical science. This interdisciplinary structure enables us to approach complex research questions from multiple perspectives while fostering collaboration across conventional disciplinary boundaries.

## Innovative Electrochemistry

Electrochemistry is experiencing renewed importance as a powerful approach for investigating chemical, physical, and biological systems. Its integration with materials science, mathematics, statistics, data science, and informatics continues to expand both the scope and precision of electrochemical research.

Advances in areas such as ultramicroelectrodes, electrochemical sensing, scanning electrochemical microscopy, functional electrode materials, and spatially resolved electrochemical analysis are creating new opportunities to investigate interfacial processes with increasingly high temporal and spatial resolution.

At Universitas Diponegoro, RCEC seeks to contribute to these emerging directions by developing electrochemical methods, materials, and analytical approaches that deepen our understanding of fundamental processes while enabling applications in areas such as energy, health, environmental monitoring, biotechnology, and advanced materials. Through collaborative and interdisciplinary research, we aim to strengthen electrochemical science as both a fundamental field of inquiry and a platform for addressing contemporary scientific and technological challenges.
