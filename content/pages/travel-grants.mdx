---
title: "Travel Grants"
---

<Text>

Bard CEC offers $500 travel grants to support affiliated students and postdoctoral researchers in attending electrochemistry-focused conferences (e.g., Gordon Research Conferences, Pittcon, and ECS meetings). These awards enhance the visibility of the Bard CEC and highlight UT’s leadership in electrochemistry research. The scope of the Bard CEC travel awards has recently expanded to include other essential research travel, such as visits for specialized instrumentation, training courses, workshops, and related research activities. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis with no fixed deadlines; applicants are encouraged to complete [the application form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSd1UaOiQ6FtiwYO2U1oZviLcxr_EBB02PN2-o-sLtBIJ5WRUg/viewform?usp=header) prior to their planned travel.

</Text>
